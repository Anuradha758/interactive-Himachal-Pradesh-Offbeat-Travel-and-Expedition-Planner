A travel guide to Himachal Pradesh, India

Mountains that keep their own time.

Five great ranges, trails from apple orchards to 5,000-metre passes, and a slower way to see them.

Himachal is not one landscape but three stacked on top of each other. Green valleys at the base, narrow ridges above them, and bare high passes where the road gives out and the sky takes over.

Valleys

350 to 2,000 m

Tea gardens, pine slopes and orchard towns like Palampur and Shimla.

Ridges

2,000 to 4,000 m

Narrow spines and meadows across the Dhauladhar and Pir Panjal.

Passes

Above 4,000 m

Rohtang, Kunzum La and Baralacha La, open for a few months a year.
